5 Signs Your Long Beach Home Has a Drain Problem Brewing

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Frans Plumbing.

Your drains work quietly in the background until they don't. If you own a home in Long Beach, here are five signs it may be time to have a plumber take a look before a slow drain turns into a backup.

1. More than one drain is slow

One slow sink is usually a local clog. When the kitchen, a bathroom, and the laundry all drain slowly, the problem is usually deeper in the line, not at the fixture.

2. Gurgling sounds

Gurgling from a drain or toilet when water runs somewhere else in the house means air is getting pushed around in the lines. That is often an early sign of a blockage forming.

3. A sewer smell that keeps coming back

A faint sewer smell near a floor drain or in a bathroom can mean a dry trap, which is a quick fix, or a vent or line problem, which is not. If it keeps returning, get it checked.

4. Water backing up in the lowest tub or shower

When a clog forms in the main line, water looks for the lowest way out. A tub or shower that fills when the washing machine drains is a classic sign.

5. Wet spots or extra-green patches in the yard

A patch of grass that is greener than the rest, or a spot that stays soggy, can point to a leaking line underground. Worth a camera look before it gets worse.
