Tankless Water Heaters in Long Beach Homes: What Changes and What It Costs You in Space

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Frans Plumbing.

A tankless water heater heats water as you use it instead of storing 40 or 50 gallons all day. For Long Beach homes with tight garages and closets, the differences are practical.

1. You get the closet back

A tankless unit mounts on the wall, about the size of a suitcase. The floor space the old tank used becomes storage again, and there is no tank of standing water to leak.

2. Hot water runs as long as the tap is open

Back to back showers stop being a scheduling problem. The unit heats on demand, so the limit is the flow rate the unit was sized for, not a tank emptying.

3. Sizing and gas lines decide the quote

The right unit depends on how many fixtures run at once, and some homes need gas line work to feed it. Frans Plumbing installs tankless water heaters across Long Beach, Lakewood, Seal Beach and Carson, and sizes the unit to the house before quoting.
