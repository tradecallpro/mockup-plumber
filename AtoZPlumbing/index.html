5 Signs Your Glendale Home Has a Hidden Leak

Sample post for illustration. Not written by A to Z Plumbing.

A hidden leak wastes water and quietly damages floors, walls, and cabinets. If you own a home in Glendale or anywhere in the Los Angeles area, here are five signs it may be time to have a plumber take a look.

1. Your water bill climbs for no clear reason

If your use has not changed but the bill keeps going up month after month, water is going somewhere. A running toilet or a leaking line is a common cause.

2. A musty smell that keeps coming back

A musty smell under a sink, in a bathroom, or near a water heater often means moisture is sitting where it should not. Cleaning hides the smell for a day. It does not fix the source.

3. Stains, bubbling paint, or warped floors

Water stains on a ceiling or wall, paint that bubbles, and floor boards that warp or feel soft all point to water getting in behind the surface.

4. You hear water when nothing is running

If you hear water moving in the pipes when every faucet is off and no appliance is filling, something is flowing that should not be.

5. Low water pressure in one part of the house

Pressure that drops in one bathroom or one part of the house can mean a leak or a failing line in that run, especially in older Glendale homes.
