Accessibility Statement

Sierra Commercial Plumbing, Inc. is committed to making this website usable for all visitors, including people with disabilities. We work toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. WCAG 2.1 AA is the technical standard commonly referenced for website accessibility under the Americans with Disabilities Act.

Measures we take

  • Pages are built with a clear heading structure and semantic markup so assistive technology can read content in a logical order.
  • Informative images carry text descriptions (alt text). Decorative images are marked so screen readers skip them.
  • Text and background color combinations meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios.
  • All interactive elements, including menus, buttons, and forms, can be reached and operated with a keyboard alone.
  • Form fields have visible labels and instructions. When a form entry has an error, the message identifies the field and what to fix.
  • Links and buttons have descriptive names that state what they do.
  • We scan the site periodically with automated accessibility tools and correct the issues the scans find.
  • New pages and features are checked against these same measures before they are published.

Known limitations

Some content may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA. This site includes content supplied by third parties, such as map embeds and review feeds. We do not control the accessibility of third-party content, and we report issues to those providers when we find them. We correct limitations on our own content as we identify them.
