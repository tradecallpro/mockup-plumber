5 Signs Your Burbank Home Has a Sewer Line Problem

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Dr. Drain.

Your sewer line works quietly underground until it doesn't. If you own a home in Burbank, here are five signs it may be time to have a drain specialist take a look before a slow line turns into a backup.

1. More than one drain is slow

One slow sink is usually a local clog. When several drains across the house slow down together, the problem usually sits in the main line, not at the fixture.

2. Gurgling from drains or toilets

Gurgling when water runs somewhere else in the house means air is being pushed around in the lines. It is often the first sound a forming blockage makes.

3. A sewer smell indoors

A sewer smell near a floor drain or in a bathroom can be a dry trap, which is simple, or a vent or line problem, which is not. If it keeps coming back, get it looked at.

4. Backups in the lowest tub or shower

When the main line blocks, water finds the lowest way out. A tub that fills when the washing machine drains is the classic warning sign.

5. Soggy or extra-green patches in the yard

A patch of lawn that stays wet, or grows greener than the rest, can sit right over a leaking sewer line. A camera inspection settles it one way or the other.
