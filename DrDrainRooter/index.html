Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking: Which One Does Your Burbank Drain Actually Need?

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Dr. Drain Rooter and Hydro Jett.

Both methods clear drains, but they do different work. Picking the right one is the difference between a line that stays clear and a clog that returns in three months.

1. Snaking opens a hole

A cable machine punches through the blockage and restores flow. It is fast and right for a single clog, but it leaves grease and scale on the pipe walls for the next clog to grab onto.

2. Jetting cleans the whole pipe

Hydro jetting scours the full inside of the line with high pressure water, removing the buildup itself. Recurring clogs, grease lines and root hairs respond best to jetting.

3. The condition of the line decides

Old or damaged lines get inspected first, because jetting a broken pipe does not fix the break. Dr. Drain Rooter and Hydro Jett clears and jets lines across Burbank, Glendale and North Hollywood, and recommends replacement when the line itself is the problem.
