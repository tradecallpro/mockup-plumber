Repiping an Older Los Angeles Home: What the Job Actually Involves

Sample post for illustration. Not written by A A Twin Brothers Plumbing.

Many Los Angeles homes still run on pipes installed decades ago. When leaks start appearing in more than one place, a repipe often costs less than chasing repairs. Here is what the work looks like.

1. It starts under the building

Most of the main lines run under the house or building. A crew opens access points, removes the old lines, and sets new pipe in the same runs, which is how a four unit building in Culver City got all new plumbing with the job finished a day early.

2. Water stays off for hours, not days

A planned repipe is staged so the water is off for the shortest window possible, and each connection is pressure tested before anything is closed back up.

3. The signs it is time

Recurring leaks, rusty water, and pressure that keeps dropping are the usual signals. A A Twin Brothers Plumbing repipes homes and small buildings across Westchester, Culver City, Inglewood and the greater Los Angeles area.
