5 Signs Your Los Angeles Home Needs a Plumber, Not a Plunger

Sample post for illustration. Not written by AA Twin Brothers.

A plunger handles a simple clog. When the same problems keep coming back, the issue usually sits deeper in the system. If you own a home in Los Angeles, here are five signs it is time to call a plumber instead.

1. The same drain keeps clogging

A drain that clogs every few weeks is telling you something. Buildup, roots, or a damaged line keep narrowing the pipe, and clearing it only buys a little time.

2. Low water pressure

Pressure that drops across the house can point to a leak, corroded lines, or a failing pressure regulator. One weak faucet is a fixture problem; a whole weak house is a system problem.

3. Rusty or discolored water

Brown or yellow water from the hot side often means the water heater is corroding inside. From both sides, older galvanized lines are usually the cause.

4. A water bill that keeps creeping up

A bill that rises with no change in use usually means water is going somewhere it should not: a running toilet, a slab leak, or a line underground.

5. Gurgling or sewer smells

Gurgling drains and sewer smells point to vent or drain line trouble. Both get worse, not better, and both are cheaper to fix early.
