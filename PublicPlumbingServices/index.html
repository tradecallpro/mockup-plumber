5 Signs Your Azusa Home Has a Water Heater Problem

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Public Plumbing Services.

Your water heater works quietly in a corner until it doesn't. If you own a home in Azusa or anywhere in the San Gabriel Valley, here are five signs it may be time to have a plumber take a look before a small problem turns into a flooded garage.

1. Rusty or cloudy hot water

Brown tint from the hot side usually means the tank is corroding inside. It is one of the clearest signs a heater is near the end of its life.

2. Rumbling or popping sounds

Noise from the tank usually means sediment has built up on the bottom. The heater works harder, heats slower, and wears out faster.

3. Water pooling at the base

A puddle under the heater can be a valve, a fitting, or the tank itself. Two of those are quick fixes; the third is not, and all three get worse.

4. Running out of hot water faster

Showers going cold sooner than they used to means the heater is losing capacity, from sediment, a failing element, or simple age.

5. An older unit that has never been serviced

A heater that has run for years with no flush or inspection is living on borrowed time. A quick service visit tells you where it stands.
