Sewer Camera Inspections in the San Gabriel Valley: What Homeowners Need to Know

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Public Plumbing Services.

A sewer backup gets everyone’s attention, but the expensive mistakes usually happen before the repair, when a line gets replaced without anyone seeing inside it first. A camera inspection is the short step that tells you what is actually wrong. Here is what it covers.

1. You see the problem before you pay to fix it

The camera shows roots, scale, cracks and bellies in the line on a screen, with the location measured. A repair quote can then be tied to a specific problem at a specific spot, instead of a guess about the whole line.

2. Small problems get caught while they are cheap

Root intrusion and descaling needs show up on camera long before a full backup. Clearing and treating a line early costs a fraction of an emergency replacement later.

3. The repair method follows the evidence

Some lines are good candidates for trenchless lining. Others need a section replaced. Public Plumbing Services runs camera inspections across the San Gabriel Valley, including Azusa, Glendora, Covina, San Dimas, Duarte, Monrovia and Arcadia, and recommends the method the footage supports.
