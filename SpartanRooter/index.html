Signs Your Cypress Sewer Line Needs Attention Before It Backs Up

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Spartan Rooter.

A sewer line rarely fails without warning. The signs show up weeks or months earlier, in drains and yards all over the house. Here is what to watch for.

1. More than one drain is slow

One slow drain is usually a local clog. When a tub, a toilet and a sink all drain slowly at the same time, the problem is often in the main line they share.

2. Gurgling and sewer smells

Gurgling toilets and sewer odors around floor drains mean air and gas are moving the wrong way through the system. That points to a blockage or a break in the line.

3. Roots and older pipes

Tree roots find the joints in older sewer lines and grow into them. Spartan Rooter clears root blockages with rooter service across Cypress, Los Alamitos, Seal Beach and Garden Grove, and can tell you whether clearing is enough or the line needs repair.
